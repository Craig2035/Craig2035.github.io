###### Data in Education

**Executive summary:**

A showcase of how data can transform educational decision‑making, pastoral insight, and operational planning.  
This project demonstrates how GCSE results, student demographics, attendance, and progress data can be combined into a clear, actionable reporting model for school leaders.

---

### High School GCSE Tracking and Results Dashboard

**Summary of Project**

Annual tracking and reviewing of GCSE results in UK schools has often relied on spreadsheets or exam‑board‑specific tools.  
Using Power BI, I created a multi‑page dashboard model that provides:

- Whole‑school executive overview  
- Subject‑level performance with filtering by gender, SEND, EAL, and Pupil Premium  
- Subject comparison and ranking  
- Individual student performance for tailored reporting  

**Note:**  
All student names and data were AI‑generated to represent a normally distributed cohort from a non‑selective school.

### Dashboard 1: Overview

![Overview Dashboard](/assets/images/GCSE_overview.png)

Impact scores of results are presented in a series of KPI cards at the top of the page. The main column chart presents distribution of whole school results, while progress through mock series is tracked in the line chart bottom left.
